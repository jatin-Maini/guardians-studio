Privacy Policy

App: Guardians of the Elements
Developer: Guardians Studio
Effective date: October 6, 2026

Guardians Studio ("Guardians Studio", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, stored, used, and shared when you use Guardians of the Elements (the "Game").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account and authentication information

The Game can create an anonymous Firebase account so that game data can be associated with a unique account identifier. If you choose to use Google sign-in or link a Google account, Firebase may provide us with information associated with that account, such as:

  • Firebase user ID (UID)
  • Google account display name
  • Google account email address
  • Profile photo URL
  • Google/Firebase provider identifiers

Google sign-in is optional. You can play the Game anonymously where supported by the current version of the Game.

1.2 Game progress, settings, and gameplay data

We store game-related information needed to provide the Game, maintain your progress, and support cloud synchronization. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Level and world progression
  • Star ratings and completion information
  • Coin and other in-game economy information
  • Power-up information
  • Tutorial and onboarding state
  • World-completion information
  • Game settings such as music, sound effects, and haptics
  • Information needed to maintain purchase records and entitlements

1.3 Analytics and app activity

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is used and to improve gameplay and the overall product. Analytics may include events such as game sessions, level starts and completions, level failures, moves, clusters, power-up use, world selection/unlocking, shop activity, coin-related events, and rewarded-ad lifecycle events.

Firebase Analytics may also collect standard information automatically when analytics collection is enabled, such as app/session information and device or app information.

1.4 Crash and diagnostic information

The Game currently includes Firebase Crashlytics. Crashlytics may collect technical information associated with crashes and errors, such as crash stack traces, device information, operating-system information, app version information, and related identifiers. This information is used to diagnose problems, improve stability, and maintain the Game.

1.5 Advertising information

The Game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide rewarded advertisements. AdMob may process information associated with advertising delivery, including advertising identifiers and device/app signals, subject to Google's services and applicable settings.

The Game currently includes Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) library, but the Game's current consent implementation is not yet a full UMP consent flow. We will update this Privacy Policy when the consent implementation is changed.

1.6 Purchases

The Game supports in-app purchases through Unity Purchasing and Google Play Billing. We may receive information such as the purchased product, transaction identifier, purchase/ownership status, and an account identifier used to associate the purchase with the Game account.

We do not receive or store your full payment-card number or other complete payment instrument details. Payments are processed by Google Play under Google's applicable terms and policies.

2. Where Information Is Stored

2.1 Local storage

Game progress, settings, economy information, advertising-related local counters, session information, and certain purchase/account records may be stored locally on your device.

2.2 Cloud storage

When cloud synchronization is used, the Game stores game-save information with Firebase Firestore. Cloud-save information is associated with your Firebase user ID. The cloud save may contain profile display-name information, progression, economy, power-up, tutorial, settings, and world-completion information.

3. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes including:

  • Providing and maintaining game functionality
  • Saving and synchronizing game progress
  • Managing accounts and authentication
  • Processing and maintaining in-app purchase entitlements
  • Providing rewarded advertisements
  • Understanding gameplay and improving the Game
  • Diagnosing crashes, bugs, and technical problems
  • Maintaining security and preventing misuse or fraud associated with purchases and accounts

4. Service Providers and Data Sharing

We use third-party services that process information on our behalf or as part of providing their services:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, cloud storage, analytics, and crash diagnostics.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — rewarded advertising.
  • Google Play — app distribution and in-app purchase processing.
  • Google sign-in services — optional account authentication/linking.

We do not currently use third-party advertising mediation networks or mobile measurement/attribution services such as AppsFlyer or Adjust.

5. Information We Do Not Collect Through the Game

Based on the current Game implementation, the Game does not request or use permissions for precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, health information, or message content.

We do not collect your payment-card details through the Game.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide the Game, maintain your account and purchases, operate our services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

Some information is stored locally and may be removed when local game data is reset or the application is uninstalled. Resetting game progress does not necessarily mean that information held by third-party services such as Firebase or Google Play has been deleted.

If you want to request deletion of information associated with your Game account, contact us at reachus.guardiansstudio@gmail.com. We may need to verify the request and may retain information where required or permitted by law, or where necessary to maintain legitimate purchase, security, or legal records.

7. Your Choices

  • You may choose whether to link the Game to a Google account where the feature is offered.
  • You may use platform and device controls relating to advertising and privacy where available.
  • You may contact us to ask about your personal information or request deletion, subject to applicable requirements and limitations.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children for purposes unrelated to providing the Game. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information in a way that requires deletion, please contact us at reachus.guardiansstudio@gmail.com.

9. International Data Transfers

Our third-party service providers, including Google services, may process information on servers located in countries other than the country in which you live. Such processing is subject to the providers' applicable terms, privacy practices, and legal safeguards.

10. Security

We use the security mechanisms provided by our service providers and the platform infrastructure used by the Game. Data transmitted to services such as Firebase and Google services is handled using their applicable secure transport and security mechanisms. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its services, or applicable legal requirements change. We will update the effective date at the top of this page when material changes are made.

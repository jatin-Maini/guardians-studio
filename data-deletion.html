Delete Your Guardians of the Elements Account

Guardians Studio provides a way to request deletion of your Guardians of the Elements account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Email reachus.guardiansstudio@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Google account, where applicable.
  2. Use the subject line Guardians of the Elements — Account Deletion Request.
  3. Tell us that you want your Guardians of the Elements account and associated personal data deleted. If you know your in-game/Firebase user ID, you may include it to help us locate the account.

What will be deleted

Subject to applicable legal requirements, we will delete or request deletion of personal information associated with the account that we control, including the Firebase account identifier and associated cloud-save/profile information. Cloud-save information may include display name, game progression, economy information, power-ups, tutorial state, settings, and world-completion information.

What may be retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required or permitted by law, security requirements, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate purchase/account records. Information held directly by Google Play or other third-party service providers is subject to their own applicable deletion processes and policies.

Processing time

We will review and process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period and will communicate with you if additional information is required to verify the request.

Important: Resetting game progress inside the Game is not the same as deleting your account or deleting data held by third-party services.
